Audio project

Made by Journey Crittendon

This audio project is about me coming home from a night out at around 2am, very tipsy and starting to regret it. I used about 11 audio samples, not including my own. I didn’t use many effects except for the samples that I recorded myself to help everything blend together; playing with the volume and muffling. I used the splitting of audio clips, layering and continued to play with the volume. I really enjoyed this audio project, and it makes me want to experiment with the usage of audio even further.

Made with Audacity.
