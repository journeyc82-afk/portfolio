animation project

By Journey crittendon

I decided to animate the feeling of annoyance. I thought of how crowded New York is, how there’s so many people that it’s difficult to get around. A huge theme in the city is slow walkers, so I used some “wiggle” animations on the slow walker, to show that they’re at their own pace without care. I also used “position” on the trees so they can move at the same pace as the slow walker, that way the focus is more on them. I used “rotate” and “position” on the fast walker to show it was slightly annoyed by the slow pace in front of it. I used all animation properties for every vector image including “opacity”, I personally felt like it helped with aesthetics. The most challenging part of the project was keeping track of all the keyframes on such a small screen (my macbook). As well as keeping the right timing for all the keyframes. When the process felt frustrating, I would simply tell myself to chill out. I’d step away, look at a cheat sheet, and then continue.

Made with Adobe llustrator and After Affects.
